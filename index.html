The Sacred Seven Vows (Saat Pheras)

Seven sacred promises made around the holy Agni for seven lifetimes of togetherness

1

ॐ एकमिषे विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Nourishment & Wellbeing

To provide sustenance, health, and a pure, joyful environment for our home and family.

2

द्वे ऊर्जे विष्णुस्त्वान्वेतु

Mental & Physical Strength

To stand as each other’s greatest pillar of courage, resilience, and unconditional protection.

3

त्रीणि रायस्पोषाय

Prosperity & Righteousness

To earn wealth through righteous virtue, share prosperity, and uphold spiritual wisdom.

4

चत्वारि मायोभवाय

Happiness & Harmony

To fill our household with laughter, mutual respect, sacred traditions, and profound inner peace.

5

पञ्च पशुभ्यो

Compassion & Progeny

To bless our future generations with noble virtues, wisdom, kindness, and eternal love.

6

षड् ऋतुकल्पाय

Everlasting Seasons of Joy

To walk through all seasons of life with unwavering companionship, grace, and devotion.

7

सखे सप्तपदा भव

Eternal Friendship & Soul Union

Now we are true companions for all eternity. May our two souls remain inseparable across lifetimes.
